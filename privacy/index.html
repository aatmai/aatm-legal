Privacy Policy

aatm · Last updated 29 August 2026 · hello@aatm.ai

aatm is a Vedic astrology companion. This policy says, in plain language, what data aatm holds, why, and what control you have. The short version: your data exists to read your own chart back to you, it is never sold, there are no ads, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

What we collect

What you give us directly

  • Your name (as entered at onboarding), and the birth details you enter: date of birth, time of birth (or a time window if you are unsure), and place of birth. Place is what you type, never your device's location. aatm has no access to your device location.
  • Your conversations with aatm (the Ask thread), including decisions you ask aatm to hold, reflections you write, and the verifications or corrections you give ("that landed", "not really", and similar).
  • If you add a person to read a relationship: the name, relation, and birth details you enter for them. This is your private lens only. The person is never notified, never contacted, and never looked up anywhere; we store only what you typed.

What is collected automatically

  • App interaction analytics (which screens are used, feature events) via Google Firebase Analytics, associated with a device identifier (the Android advertising ID). Used only to understand and improve the product. We show no ads.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics via Firebase Crashlytics, to fix failures.

What stays on your phone

  • If you use voice dictation, the audio is processed on your device. Audio is not uploaded to our servers.

What we do with it

Your birth details are used to compute your chart and its time periods. Your conversations and verifications are used to compose your readings, your record, and the continuity that makes aatm remember what you told it. Aggregated, de-identified usage data helps us improve the product. That is the whole list.

Specifically, we do not: sell or rent your data to anyone; show advertising; use your data to train models shared outside your own account's experience; or contact anyone you add to the app.

Where it lives and how it moves

Your data is stored on our servers and transmitted only over encrypted connections (HTTPS; cleartext traffic is disabled in the app). Sign-in is through Google; we receive your Google account email for authentication and support.

Your controls

  • Export. You can export everything aatm holds about you from inside the app. The export includes your profile, birth details, conversations, decisions, verifications, reflections, and any people you added.
  • Delete. Settings › Delete everything removes your data, including your sign-in record. Deletion is immediate and total on our side. You can also request deletion without the app at this page.
  • Quiet. aatm only initiates contact through in-app check-ins you have consented to, at a cadence you chose, and you can turn that off entirely. There are no push notifications.

What aatm is not

aatm offers orientation, not outcomes. It is not a substitute for medical, legal, or financial advice, and nothing in it should be treated as any of those.

Children

aatm is intended for users 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will say so plainly. The current version always lives at this URL.
