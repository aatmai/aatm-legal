Write to hello@aatm.ai. A person reads every message and replies.

What helps us help you

  • Your phone (Android or iPhone) and the app version, from Settings.
  • What you were doing, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it shows the problem.

Please do not send your birth details or passwords by email. We never need them to help you.

Your data

  • A copy of your data: Settings › What aatm keeps › Export everything.
  • Turning off AI: Settings › Readings written by AI.
  • Deleting your account: Settings › Delete everything, or without the app.
