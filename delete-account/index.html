Account deletion

Delete your aatm account

aatm · Last updated 29 August 2026 · hello@aatm.ai

You can delete your aatm account and all data connected to it at any time. There are two ways.

From inside the app (immediate)

Settings › Delete everything.

This removes, immediately and permanently: your profile and birth details, your chart and all readings, your conversations with aatm, tracked decisions and their history, reflections, verifications, your record, any people you added and everything computed from their charts, and your sign-in record. There is no grace period and no recovery. aatm keeps nothing that identifies you.

Without the app (if you have uninstalled it)

Email hello@aatm.ai from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We verify that the request comes from the account owner and complete the deletion within 7 days, then confirm by reply. No reason needed, no questions asked.

What deletion covers

Everything named above, which is everything aatm stores about you. Aggregated analytics that never identified you (for example, counts of how often a screen is opened across all users) are not connected to your account and cannot be traced back to you.

Before you go

If you want a copy of your data first, use Export from inside the app before deleting. Export is unavailable after deletion because nothing remains.
